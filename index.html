Priority Focus

Sanitation While Walking – It's Non-Negotiable

Leaving waste behind harms the environment, spreads disease, and gives all dog owners a bad name. Responsible owners always carry bags and dispose of waste properly.

  • Carry at least 2–3 biodegradable bags on every walk
  • Dispose in designated bins or take home if none available
  • Clean up even in secluded areas – wildlife and waterways matter
  • Teach children the same habit from day one
